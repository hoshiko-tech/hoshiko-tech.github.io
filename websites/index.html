FAQ

Good questions

How long does a site take?

A one-page site is usually days, not weeks. Larger, more involved projects take longer. I'll give you a realistic timeline before we start.

Do I own the website?

Yes, completely. You own the code, the content, and all the accounts. Nothing is locked to me, and you're free to take it elsewhere anytime.

What about hosting and domains?

I'll set everything up and can recommend affordable options. Hosting and domain fees are paid directly by you, at cost, with no markup.

Can you maintain it afterwards?

Yes. I can handle updates and small changes on an as-needed basis, or set you up so you can edit content yourself.

Is this the same as Hoshiko Tech's consultancy?

It's a smaller, more affordable service run alongside it, with the same engineering standards, scoped for simple websites and side-project budgets.
